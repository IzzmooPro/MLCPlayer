MLC Player — Qt replacement and relinking information
======================================================

MLC Player v0.38 uses PyQt6 6.10.2 and the LGPL Qt 6.10.2 shared libraries.
The Qt libraries and plugins are dynamically loaded from this install path:

    _internal\PyQt6\Qt6

MLC Player does not statically link Qt.  Therefore application object files are not required
to use a modified Qt build: a compatible replacement is made
at the shared-library boundary.  MLC Player does not enforce a signature or
checksum on replacement Qt DLLs or plugins.

Replacement procedure
---------------------

1. Exit MLC Player and confirm that no MLC Player process remains.
2. Copy the complete _internal\PyQt6\Qt6 directory as a backup.
3. Build a Windows x86-64 shared-library Qt that is ABI-compatible with the
   PyQt6 6.10.2 extension modules.  Preserve the DLL and plugin names and the
   relative bin\ and plugins\ paths used by the installed package.
4. Replace the required Qt DLLs/plugins beneath _internal\PyQt6\Qt6.  Do not
   replace the PyQt6 .pyd binding modules unless you also supply a compatible
   PyQt6 build.
5. Start MLC Player from the modified copy.  Reinstalling or upgrading MLC
   Player may overwrite the replacement files, so retain your backup/build.

The exact Qt corresponding sources used for this release are distributed as:

    qtbase-everywhere-src-6.10.2.tar.xz
    qtsvg-everywhere-src-6.10.2.tar.xz
    qtimageformats-everywhere-src-6.10.2.tar.xz

The exact PyQt binding source is distributed as pyqt6-6.10.2.tar.gz.  Source
asset names, byte sizes and SHA-256 values are fixed by
packaging/corresponding_sources.json in the MLC Player source tree and the
assets are published beside the installer.
